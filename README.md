# Chip8
This is my first emulation project. <br>
I am going to share my process of building it. <br>

<h3>Story</h3>
I was playing moonring on steam, where I got the idea of playing through all the 2D Legend of Zelda games, starting from the original 1986 game. So I had to look at some emulators online of the NES, SNES, GBA etc to be able to play these games. Looking through those, I got the idea of making my own emulator as it would be a nice intersection of my interest of low level programming and video games. I also have a microprocessors and microcontrollers course going on in college, so that helps as well. Upon searching, I found that Chip 8 is the easiest emulation project I can undertake, so here we are
